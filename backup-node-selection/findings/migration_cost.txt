==============================================================================
Migration cost of real network-function state
==============================================================================

A connection-tracking flow table (flow_state.py) filled to steady state
by a flow-arrival workload (workload.py). DEFT's own backup discards the
state it receives, so none of this could be measured before.

Table occupancy at steady state
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     flows/s     timeout       entries      Little   delta/rnd   delta share
         500          30        15,656      15,075         521         3.3%
        2000          30        62,045      60,298       2,053         3.3%
        8000          30       248,272     241,193       8,214         3.3%

'Little' is the Little's-law prediction arrival_rate x (mean lifetime +
idle timeout); agreement is a check on the generator, not a second result.

xmlrpc is what DEFT puts on the wire. Its <int> is signed 32-bit, so an
IPv4 address does not fit and must be sent as text; that, plus per-value
markup, is where the inflation comes from.
