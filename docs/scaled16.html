Scaled + Heterogeneous: 16 Hosts

A 16-host network (10 primaries, 6 hosts that can act as backups), where 2 of those 6 backups are made deliberately bad (health=0.05, capacity ratio≈0.03, and an extra 50ms delay on every call). This is the test that shows whether the smarter methods really do avoid known-bad hosts, and it is the main result of this project.

5 context seeds × 3 trials 200 calls/primary, burst=10 n = 30,000 calls/method baseline excluded (9-host only)
How often each method used a bad host (out of 10 choices per seed): concentrated, heuristic, optimal, and rl never used one, in all 5 seeds. round_robin used one 2 times out of 10, in every seed, because it just goes round the pool and has no way to know which hosts are bad. random varies from 0 to 4 out of 10, depending on the seed.

Mechanism Check: Latency vs. Backup Load

All methods and seeds pooled together. The question: does sharing one backup between more primaries slow things down on its own, no matter which method caused the sharing?

Load 9 is concentrated putting 9 of its 10 primaries on one backup (the tenth is that backup's own primary, which cannot back up to itself). Average and p99 latency both jump, which fits the connection queue filling up on Python's single-threaded SimpleXMLRPCServer once enough calls arrive at one backup at the same time.

What this means: this test separates knowing which host is good from simply spreading load. round_robin spreads load as well as anything, but it cannot tell a good host from a bad one, so apart from random it is the only method that keeps using bad hosts. heuristic, optimal, and rl avoid the bad hosts completely in every seed, and their results are too close to tell apart. That the RL policy does this across every seed, not just one, shows it learned the idea rather than memorising a single case. This is the main result of the project: once hosts differ in quality, choosing a backup by its features beats spreading load blindly, and the reason why is clear from the numbers. Hosts differing in quality is the normal case in production.
